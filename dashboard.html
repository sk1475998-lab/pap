Roadmap concepts · not shipped capabilities

Product Roadmap

The cards below describe proposed capabilities, not functionality available or production-validated in this build. Current application assistance is review-first; you review and submit every application yourself.

ROADMAP 01 Tier-1 Core
Universal Resume Parser 3.0
Deep vertical Y-shift line boundary reconstruction (Math.abs(item.ty - lastY) > 3.5), top-to-bottom layout coordinate sorting, and defensive Zod preprocessors that guarantee zero data truncation across multiple degrees, jobs, and project portfolios.
PDF.js geometrical bounding-box sorting
Null-defensive salvage schema (no thrown crashes)
Dynamic skill bucket deduplication & taxonomy
Native .DOCX, PDF, and TXT parsing support
pdf-geometry zod-salvage ast-reconstruction zero-truncation
ROADMAP 02 Form Agent
Autonomous Form Solver & Shadow DOM Engine
Universal multi-step autonomous form runner engineered for tier-1 Enterprise ATS portals: Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Taleo, iCIMS, Ashby, and LinkedIn Easy Apply. Solves complex multi-page questionnaires with stall watchdogs.
Open & Closed Shadow DOM recursive piercing
Native React/Angular synthetic event dispatch
Multi-step progress tracker with 8s stall watchdog
Automated resume & cover letter binary injection
shadow-dom workday greenhouse lever ashby
ROADMAP 03 High Throughput
Fleet Multi-Job Parallel Applier
Dispatches up to 10 headless worker instances concurrently. Each worker discovers matching openings, dynamically tailors candidate resumes per requisition, answers screening questions, and executes submissions in parallel.
10 isolated background worker pipelines
Human-like gaussian jitter delays & rate safety
Master resume immutability protection
OTP / Captcha detection with instant pause alert
fleet-workers review-first isolated-sessions anti-bot
ROADMAP 04 ATS Intelligence
Real-time ATS Matcher & Keyword Scorer
Calculates a deterministic resume-readiness estimate from section structure, parsing signals, and job-description keyword coverage. It is not an employer's ATS score or a pass-probability prediction.
Resume structure and parseability checks
Required and preferred keyword coverage
Missing terms shown for review, never added as claimed skills
Before-and-after scoring uses the same deterministic analyzer
deterministic keyword-coverage semantic-diff keyword-gap
ROADMAP 05 Generative AI
Dynamic Cover Letter Synthesizer & STAR Copilot
Synthesizes role-specific, grounded cover letters and STAR-format interview answers directly from the candidate's real project achievements and the hiring company's core mission with 4 selectable executive tones.
4 Executive Tones: Professional, Concise, Storyteller, Enthusiastic
Fact-grounded generation avoiding hallucinated metrics
Automatic ATS formatting and custom PDF export
Company culture & core values alignment integration
star-framework executive-tones hallucination-guard pdf-export

ROADMAP 07 Pipeline Sync
Intelligent Email & Offer Lifecycle Tracker
Monitors candidate application email threads via IMAP/SMTP, parses employer responses, detects interview invitations, status changes, and rejections, and synchronizes the Kanban board in real time.
Automated Kanban pipeline transitions (Applied → Screen → Offer)
Calendar invite detection & interview scheduling alerts
Email sentiment classification and auto-draft follow-ups
Multi-provider enterprise mail server support
imap-smtp nlp-classifier kanban-sync interview-alert
ROADMAP 08 Career Prep
Autonomous Interview Prep Copilot
Profiles the hiring company's engineering tech stack, open-source repositories, and Glassdoor interview records to generate role-specific technical drills, STAR responses, and executive questions for the interviewer.
Role-specific system design & coding drill banks
Tailored STAR answers mapped to your verified projects
Salary negotiation playbook & counter-offer strategies
Post-round interview debrief and follow-up letter builder
tech-profiling star-answers salary-negotiation mock-interview
ROADMAP 09 Global Compliance
Multi-Locale Global Mobility & Compliance Engine
Seamlessly adapts application parameters across US (EEOC, OFCCP, H-1B, F-1 OPT/CPT, I-94), India (CTC structure, Notice Period, PAN/Aadhaar), UK (Right to Work), and EU (GDPR data protection standards).
Automated EEOC voluntary self-identification mapping
Visa sponsorship & STEM OPT extension rule engines
India CTC breakups (Fixed, Variable, Stocks, Joining Bonus)
Notice period unit harmonization (Days / Months / Buyout)
eeoc-compliance h1b-opt india-ctc global-mobility
ROADMAP 10 Agentic Core
Autonomous Crew & Model Context Protocol (MCP)
Connects SuperJobAgent directly to Claude Desktop, Cursor, and terminal coding agents using Anthropic's Model Context Protocol (MCP). Enables headless pipelines, custom scripts, and team-wide automation.
Native MCP server exposing 12 tool primitives
Role-separated research, scoring, tailoring, and review stages
Headless CLI daemon for background continuous application
Extensible plugin architecture with custom scrapers
mcp-protocol crew-ai claude-desktop cli-daemon
